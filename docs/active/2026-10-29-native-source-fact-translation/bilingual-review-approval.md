Before execution: appoint reviewers for each language; confirm independence/exposure and review access; approve rubric and denominators, exact preserved/passthrough identity rule, item sampling and clustered inference; freeze source/fact/target hashes, masking key and presentation allocation; calibrate separately, then lock rubric before evaluation. Review independently, seal initial judgments before discussion, adjudicate with evidence and preserve both originals. Missing/unassessable/pending remain explicit.

A conversion to C5 passed requires an approved versioned rule; missing judgments never imply a pass. Translation fidelity does not establish extraction correctness or clinical usefulness. Clinical utility needs a separate actual-user task study and justified sample plan. No sample size or threshold is authorized by this draft.
